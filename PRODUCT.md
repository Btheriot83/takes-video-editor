# Product

## Register

product

## Users

People capturing short vertical videos on a phone who want to record, make a few direct edits, and export without an account or an upload workflow.

## Product Purpose

Takes is a local-first camera and lightweight clip editor. Success means a new user can understand the camera controls immediately, capture several clips quickly, make essential timeline edits, and export a usable video without leaving the browser.

## Brand Personality

Familiar, focused, and calm. The interface should feel native enough to trust while remaining clearly its own product.

## Anti-references

- Do not copy Apple Clips branding, assets, proprietary iconography, or music/effects workflow.
- Do not add decorative controls, novelty interactions, or hidden gestures without a visible state or fallback.
- Do not use counterintuitive camera-direction controls or ambiguous labels.

## Design Principles

- Put the live camera and the subject first.
- Make capture physical and immediate: tap to toggle, or press and hold then release.
- Keep every camera capability honest by showing unavailable states and fallbacks.
- Preserve a short path from capture to timeline to export.
- Keep source media local to the device unless the user explicitly exports or shares it.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use conventional icons with accessible names, minimum 44px touch targets, visible focus and disabled states, reduced-motion support, and text/controls that meet WCAG AA contrast where they overlay live video.
